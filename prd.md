# PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT

Product Name:
Founder Falcon

Tagline:
Your Voice-Native AI Startup Cofounder

Problem Statement:

Millions of aspiring founders have ideas but lack access to experienced mentors, product managers, and technical advisors.

Most startup validation tools are text-heavy and impersonal.

Users need a conversational partner that can challenge assumptions, validate ideas, and generate execution plans.

Solution:

Founder Falcon uses real-time voice conversations powered by Murf Falcon to act as a startup cofounder.

Users speak naturally.

Founder Falcon responds through voice while simultaneously generating startup documents.

Target Users:

* Hackathon participants
* Student founders
* Indie hackers
* Startup teams
* Entrepreneurs
* Product managers

Core Features:

1. Voice Conversation

Users communicate entirely through voice.

2. Startup Validation

Analyze:

* Problem
* Customer
* Market
* Competition

Generate:

* Validation score

3. PRD Generator

Automatically create:

* Vision
* Goals
* Features
* User stories

4. TDS Generator

Generate:

* Architecture
* APIs
* Database design
* Deployment strategy

5. MVP Planner

Generate:

* Development roadmap
* Sprint plan
* Priorities

6. Pitch Coach

Review startup pitches and provide spoken feedback.

Success Metrics:

* Session Completion Rate

* Voice Interaction Duration
